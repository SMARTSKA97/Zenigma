Hints and Check

  • Hints work in Practice only, and one pool of hints is shared by every game.
  • Tap Hint to light up the area worth looking at, with a short reason. If something you placed cannot be right, the hint shows the area it is in (orange). Tap Hint again on the same board and you get a stronger one: the exact cell and what to do with it.
  • Check is free. It only tells you whether the board has a mistake, not where.
  • A mistake is counted only when a wrong move stays on the board for a moment, so changing a cell or taking a move back straight away costs nothing.
  • A hint costs 1. You start with 3. Spin the daily wheel on the Practice tab for 1 to 3 more, earn one on every 10th level, and collect more from badges. You can keep up to 6 from free sources.
  • A level solved with a hint can still earn up to 2 stars.

Streaks and freezes

  • Solve a game’s daily on consecutive days to build its streak.
  • Play a game on 7 different days to earn one streak freeze for that game. Each game holds up to 2.
  • A freeze covers one missed day for that game automatically. Tap the freezes chip on Home or Daily to see what each game has.
