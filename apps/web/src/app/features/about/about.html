What is inside

Word Guess, Crowns, Eclipse, Sudoku (classic and mini), Zipline and Patchwork, with more on the way. Every day brings one puzzle per game, the same for everyone, resetting at midnight India time. Practice has endless levels, and every level is identical for every player.

Fair play

Daily puzzles are timed by the server and can be started once. Solutions are checked on the server against the rules of the puzzle, so leaderboards are honest. Practice is yours to play at your own pace, offline.
