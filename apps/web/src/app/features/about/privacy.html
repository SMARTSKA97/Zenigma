Privacy policy

Last updated 12 October 2026

The short version

Zenigma collects only what it needs to run accounts, daily puzzles and leaderboards. There are no ads, no advertising or analytics trackers, and your data is never sold. You can play as a guest without giving us anything.

Guest mode

Without an account, your progress, settings and results stay on your device. Nothing about you is sent to our server, apart from asking it for the daily puzzle, which does not identify you.

What we collect with an account

  • Your email address, display name and a salted hash of your password (we cannot read your password).
  • A random device ID, the type of device (web or Android) and when it was last used, so only one device plays a daily at a time.
  • Your game results: daily attempts and times, practice levels you solved, streaks and streak freezes.
  • Circles you create or join, with their names and members' display names.

Your display name, times and ranks appear on leaderboards, and on private circles you join. Your email address is never shown to other players. The home screen shows only totals of players and recent activity.

What we do not collect

No location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or advertising ID. No third-party analytics or crash-reporting services.

On your device

  • Settings and unfinished boards are saved locally so the app works offline.
  • Android: the optional daily reminder is scheduled on your phone and sends nothing anywhere. Vibration uses the phone's vibrator only.
  • Updates (Android): the app asks GitHub for the latest release, so GitHub sees your IP address as with any download. Installing is always your choice.

Where data lives

The web app is served by Cloudflare. The API runs on Render and the database is hosted by Neon. These providers process data on our behalf to run the service, and apply their own security practices.

Your choices

  • Delete your account any time in Settings, Manage account, Delete my account. Your account, results, streaks and circles you own are removed.
  • Signing out clears your data from that device.
  • Ask questions or requests through GitHub issues.

Children

Zenigma is a general puzzle app and is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child created an account, ask us to delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes and the version history will say so.
