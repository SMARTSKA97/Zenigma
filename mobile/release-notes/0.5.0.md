## Sudoku
- Mini Sudoku is now classic 9 by 9 Sudoku, with pencil notes and a keypad that shows how many of each number are left
- 200 new levels that get harder as you go, and a new daily puzzle

## Boards
- Fixed uneven rows on the Queens and Tango grids
- Bigger boards, with a side panel for the timer and tools on wide screens
- Queens follows the original: tap for a cross, tap again for a queen
