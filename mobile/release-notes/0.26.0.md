Includes everything from 0.25.0.

## Fixed
- The daily streak no longer sticks at 1 day: puzzle days are noted right after a daily is solved and restored from your last five weeks of results.

## New
- Every share (result cards, streaks, mind set, puzzles) now ends with the app link.

## Security
- The server limits how fast results, coins and sync can be sent, per player, and can be locked to Cloudflare only.

## Account
- Signed-in devices: see every device your account is signed in on, sign one out, sign out all the others, or sign out everywhere. A free account can be signed in on 1 device, a supporter on 5. Signing in on a new device when the account is full asks which device to sign out.
- Leaderboards, Practice: a new "Fastest by level" view for Queens, Tango, Sudoku and the other grid puzzles. Pick a level (and a Sudoku kind) to see the three fastest on a podium, then the rest, with your own rank. Word Guess has "Fewest guesses": the best average guesses per word. Only solves without hints are timed.
- Settings: "Check server connection" tests whether this device can reach the server and says what is stopping it if not.
- Extra dailies: "Play next" now opens the next game fresh, instead of showing the last game's finished screen and looping between two games.
- Comet Slice: new look. A dusk-purple sky with twinkling stars, a cratered planet surface that turns grey and cracked after a skull, a white blade swipe with sparkles, and a flash of light beams when a skull goes off. A skull now ends the run on a "You lost!" scene with your score and a Continue button.
- Comet Slice: every level now speeds up as the run goes on (comets fall and arrive faster the longer you last), gently on Easy and more on Hard.
- Comet Slice: comets now have smaller heads with longer tails and skulls are a bit smaller, like the reference. Touch is easier: a finger gets a little extra reach on comets, fast swipes no longer skip comets on iPhone, a tap shows a small ripple, and the blade trail is shorter.
- iPhone and web: the server connection no longer hangs on "Waking the server". API calls now skip the service worker, a server that is awake but refusing the app stops the waiting at once, and the "Can't reach the server" notice says why.
