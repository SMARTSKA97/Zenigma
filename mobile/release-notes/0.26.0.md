Includes everything from 0.25.0.

## Fixed
- The daily streak no longer sticks at 1 day: puzzle days are noted right after a daily is solved and restored from your last five weeks of results.

## New
- Every share (result cards, streaks, mind set, puzzles) now ends with the app link.

## Security
- The server limits how fast results, coins and sync can be sent, per player, and can be locked to Cloudflare only.

## Account
- Signed-in devices: see every device your account is signed in on, sign one out, sign out all the others, or sign out everywhere. A free account can be signed in on 1 device, a supporter on 5. Signing in on a new device when the account is full asks which device to sign out.
