## Email
- Forgot your password? Get a 6-digit code by email and set a new one. You can also confirm your email in Account.

## Playing
- Messages such as "Not quite" now show in a fixed strip under the board, so the board no longer jumps.
- Queens and Tango wait a little longer before flagging a mistake, so you can fix it first.
- Results you play offline now go out on their own: shortly after you stop playing, when you leave or reopen the app, and again with growing pauses if the server cannot be reached.
- Saves only wake the server when it is already awake or when you open Ranks, Daily or Circles, so the app starts faster and results still sync.

## Hints and feel
- Hints now work in the daily challenge too, for now with no penalty. In the daily puzzles a hint works without a connection and is reported when you finish; in Word Guess daily the hint needs a connection, because the answer lives on the server.
- Word Guess: key presses and each tile flip now buzz, and animations are no longer switched off by your phone's own animation setting (use Reduce motion in Settings instead).
- Patches boxes keep an even gap and no longer touch.

## New game
- Chain Link (Mind, Language): a word is given and the next ones show only their first letter and one line per letter. Each must join the word before it into a compound, like MAN and MADE (man-made), and the one after it. Chains grow from 2 to 6 words. Three lives.

- Pinpoint (Words): five words share something in common, shown one at a time, vaguest first. Name what links them: 5 points on the first clue, down to 1. A wrong guess shows the next clue. Five categories a game, about 75 to start with.

- Crossclimb (Words): a word ladder. The first and last words are given; fill the rows between, changing one letter at a time. Any real word that keeps the way open counts. Ladders grow from 2 rows to 5.
- Wend (Words): like LinkedIn's. A grid with grey blocks hides several words of 3 to 11 letters; trace each through touching tiles (up, down, left, right), using every letter exactly once. Every board has one solution, so a real word on the wrong tiles is turned down. The board follows the day of the week, as on LinkedIn: Monday is a small 5 by 5 with four words, and it grows through the week to 7 by 7 with five or six words on Sunday. Empty slots under the grid show each word's length and fill as you find them. Hint shows the first letter of the shortest word left, then the next each time you press it again. Undo and Reset. Practice is an 8-minute run of boards that start small and grow.

- Comet Slice (Mind, Speed): a full-screen game. Comets and skulls fall through a night sky with a slow aurora, drifting stars and dark hills, each at its own speed, and everything gets faster the longer you last, like Magic Tiles. Press and drag (finger or mouse) through the comets to collect them with a glowing swipe and a firm tap of vibration; several in one swipe is a combo, shown big on screen. A comet that reaches the ground costs a heart (three hearts), with a red glow, a shake and a buzz. Slice a skull and the run is over at once, with a blast and a huge vibration. A 3-2-1 starts every run, a corner button leaves it, and the page behind never scrolls or goes back while you swipe. Practice has no levels and no time limit (a clock counts up): beat your own best. Its daily is one one-minute run with the same throws for everyone, and it is not part of the daily mind set.

## Look and feel
- The Android app now looks like the web app: the same floating tab bar at the bottom, cards, buttons and switches (the separate Material style is gone). The status bar icons follow the app's light or dark theme.
- Even margins everywhere: game boards keep about 15px either side instead of nearly touching the edge, and rows of chips scroll out to the screen edge.
- Dark theme fixes: the extra-daily screens (Wend, Comet Slice and others), the animated demos, the rules pages and a few icons were pale or hard to read at night.
- Nothing can push a page sideways any more.
- Result cards show "1 point" rather than "1 points".

## Games with letters
- Pinpoint, Chain Link and Crossclimb no longer open your phone's keyboard, which pushed the page about and made it scroll. They have their own keyboard built in at the bottom of the screen (a computer keyboard works too), and the whole game fits the screen.
- Crossclimb is quicker: each new row starts as a copy of the word above. Tap the tile you want to change and type one letter.
- Chain Link and Pinpoint show what you type right in the tiles and the answer box.
- Wend is redone: a full-screen board that fits any phone with no scrolling, a line drawn through the tiles as you trace, colours for found words that work in dark mode, and the buttons pinned to the bottom. Dragging on the board no longer scrolls the page.
- Word Guess keeps the page still while you play.
- Finishing or leaving any game, daily or practice, takes you back to the page you opened it from (the extra dailies used to land in Settings).

## Daily mind set and streak
- The daily mind set is now six games, one picked at random from each skill: memory, attention, speed, math, language and logic. The same six for everyone each day, with up to 600 points; the Mind leaderboard is out of 600.
- A new daily streak (Home and Challenges): a day counts only when you finish both the six-game mind set and all of the day's puzzles. Both halves show as ticks, and the streak stays alive until the day ends. The per-game puzzle streaks and freezes work as before.

## Faster boards
- Ranks open instantly with the last board you saw and refresh quietly, instead of a loader. A board loaded in the last minute is reused, so the server is not asked (or woken) again.

- Home opens straight away with your last streak and week, and the community numbers, without waking the server. They refresh quietly once it is awake. Badges work the same way.
- Boards, stats and the daily hub send nothing when they have not changed, which saves data.

## Waking up
- When the server is waking, screens that need it show a clear loading cover instead of a half-loaded page.
- Daily puzzles open by themselves: no more "Start today's puzzle" button.

## Reminders
- The app now asks once whether to allow notifications (and explains why first). You can change it in Settings.
- Streak alert: with reminders on, if a streak is still alive and today's puzzle is not done, you get one warning 3 hours before the next puzzles arrive. Turn it off in Settings.

## Widget
- Home-screen streak widgets in three sizes: small, medium and wide (long-press your home screen, Widgets, Zenigma): your streak, and how many of today's puzzles are done. It turns amber when your streak needs a puzzle today.

## Security
- Stronger sign-in protection: sessions end when you change your password, stolen sign-in tokens are detected, and backups of app data are off.

- Extra dailies: Pinpoint, Chain Link, Crossclimb, Wend and Comet Slice each have a daily, the same for everyone, in the Puzzles tab of Challenges. One result a day (out of 100), works offline, with a Today board when signed in. The board is for fun, as results come from the app.

## How to play
- Every game now has an animated demo (like a short video, but inside the app so it works offline): a small practice board, a finger that moves to the cells being used, captions, and highlighted "If this happens, do this" tips for tricky moments. Play, pause, step and replay. Open it from How to play in Settings (Mind games are listed there now), from the Watch the demo button on a Mind game's start screen, or from the help sheet in the puzzle games.

## Behind the scenes
- Results sent from several devices and long offline gaps are handled more carefully (the sync no longer loops when nothing new arrives), with new tests for it.
- Database indexes and clean-up of old sync events keep boards fast; a load-test script and an operations guide are included for the server.
