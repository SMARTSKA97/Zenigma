## New
- New game: Pocket Cricket, a retro-style cricket game in Mind. Bat from behind: every ball comes at a random speed; tap Swing as it reaches the bat, and miss and you are out. Hit it and the view cuts to the field: press Run for runs before the fielder's throw is back, or get caught or run out. Fours and sixes for great timing. Five outs end the game; your runs are the score.
- Comet Slice is overhauled: no speed readout and no ramp-up. Every throw is random: singles, pairs, fans of comets, rain across the screen, crossing streams, swarms, and skulls hiding among comets. Comets now come in three sizes (smaller ones are quicker and worth more), and every comet falls at its own pace. Pick Easy, Medium or Hard before a practice run; the daily gets harder through the week.
- Dailies: Comet Slice results are shown in points; the daily mind set has at most one multi-round game; "Play next" now moves forward through what is left instead of bouncing between two unplayed games.
- The web app updates itself: a new version is picked up within moments of opening or returning to the app, with no hard refresh.
- The server is woken as soon as you open the home screen.

## Fixed
- Comet Slice is no longer cut off or shifted on wide screens.
- Snappier, lighter animations (no screen-blocking page transitions, shorter list entrances) so taps are not missed while things move.
- Typing and touch feel snappier on Android (a tap-blocking workaround for iPhone zoom was swallowing quick taps; removed).

Includes everything from 0.24.0.

- Keys, grid cells and tiles in Word Guess, Tango, Sudoku and the quizzes now react the moment you touch them (no more pressing hard or twice).
- If the app ever gets stuck on an old copy, it now clears its saved copy and reloads itself once. Settings also has a "Reload fresh" button.
- "Play next" after a daily now always moves forward through today's list (puzzles, mind set, extras) and never bounces between two games.
- The daily streak no longer sticks at 1 day: puzzle days are noted right after a daily is solved and restored from your last five weeks of results.
- Every share (result cards, streaks, mind set, puzzles) now ends with the app link.
