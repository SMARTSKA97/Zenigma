## New
- New game: Pocket Cricket, a retro-style cricket game in Mind. Bat from behind: every ball comes at a random speed; tap Swing as it reaches the bat, and miss and you are out. Hit it and the view cuts to the field: press Run for runs before the fielder's throw is back, or get caught or run out. Fours and sixes for great timing. Five outs end the game; your runs are the score.
- Comet Slice is overhauled: no speed readout and no ramp-up. Every throw is random: singles, pairs, fans of comets, rain across the screen, crossing streams, swarms, and skulls hiding among comets. Comets now come in three sizes (smaller ones are quicker and worth more), and every comet falls at its own pace. Pick Easy, Medium or Hard before a practice run; the daily gets harder through the week.

## Fixed
- Typing and touch feel snappier on Android (a tap-blocking workaround for iPhone zoom was swallowing quick taps; removed).

Includes everything from 0.24.0.
