## New game: Zip
- Draw one path through every cell, passing the numbers in order from 1 to the highest
- Drag to draw, drag back to take a step back, or press a cell on the path to cut it back there
- 200 practice levels, a daily Zip with a leaderboard and streak, and it grows harder through the week
- Arrow keys work too on a keyboard
