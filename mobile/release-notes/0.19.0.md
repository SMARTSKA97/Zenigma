## New
- **Challenges** replaces Daily. One place for today's puzzles, today's mind set and your weekly, weekend and monthly goals, in three tabs.
- **Daily mind set**: three short games, one for each of three skills, the same for everyone. Finish all three to keep a mind streak.
- **Mind dashboard**: a brain map of your six skills, a radar chart, and a comparison with other players by age group and gender. Sharing is opt-in and a group needs five players before any average is shown.
- **Profile**: add a display name, age group and gender, choose what you share, and sign in with Google as well as email (web).
- **Offline mode**: a switch in Settings. On, you can play and practise anywhere and only the daily challenges pause. Off, the app wakes the server and syncs your progress.
- **Warm-up loader**: the free server sleeps when idle. The app now says it is waking and lets you carry on meanwhile.
- **Server hint balance**: your hints follow you between devices.
- **Tiers for every logic game**: Classic and Mini now have five difficulties each, and the same Easy to Insane tiers now apply to Queens, Tango, Zip and Patches.
- 1000 new levels per track every week, now across all the new tiers.

- **Zip: Walls and Blocked cells**: two new kinds. Walls the path may not cross, and blocked cells to go around. 1000 levels each.
- **Patches: Shapes only**: many numbers are hidden, so each clue shows just the shape and you work out the size.
- **Five new mind games**: Mental Turn (rotate, don't flip), Number Sort, Dot Count, Opposites and Word Recall. Mind training now has 20 games, each drawn for the skill it trains.

## Improved
- **Patches looks like LinkedIn's**: coloured clue tiles, solid pastel patches and dashed shape outlines.
- **A stray Back swipe no longer quits a game**: leaving mid-game asks first. In practice your time is paused and kept, so you carry on where you stopped.
- **Dark theme**: Windoku windows, diagonals, grid lines and the daily header are now clear in dark mode.
- **More vibration**: undo, redo, reset, pause, digits, and every answer in mind games, with a buzz for each wrong answer.
- **Smoother app**: screens load in the background so moving around is instant, and cards give a small press when tapped.
- Practice is simpler: filter by Logic, Sudoku or Words, and Mind training has its own page.
- Settings colours now fit every screen size and are unlocked from the start.
- What's new and Version history are laid out in sections: New, Improved, Fixed and Coming up.
- Source code tidied: every screen keeps its template and styles in separate files.

## Fixed
- Collecting challenge hints no longer loses the extra when your stash is at its limit of 10. You collect what fits and the rest waits for you.
- Back from a Mind game returns to Mind instead of Settings.
