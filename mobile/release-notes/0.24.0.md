## New
- **Contributors** page, always available from Settings and About: who keeps the servers running, with what they gave shown as paid (a dollar tip appears like "$5 (₹425)", converted at the rate of the day it arrived and then fixed).
- **Badge rewards**: every badge now pays 5 coins when you collect it, and the better ones pay hints too.
- Goals show a red number on the Challenges tab when a finished goal is waiting to be collected.
- Settings opens with quick cards: What's new, About, Contributors and How to play.

## Dailies
- **Extra dailies now unlock when you finish today's puzzles and the daily mind set.** They have their own Extras tab in Challenges (locked with a clear checklist until then), and each one has its own leaderboard.
- **Zipline dailies get harder through the week with walls and blocked cells:** plain on Monday, walls from Tuesday, blocked cells from Thursday, and both together by the weekend, on bigger boards.
- Practice and dailies are connected: Practice shows whether today's dailies are done, and every daily row links to that game's practice.

## Better
- Hints rebuilt: a hint now lights the exact cell, shows the answer on it and tells you why. Asking again on an unchanged board shows the same hint free, and a mistake is pointed out cell by cell. Fixed hint highlights not showing on some boards. Fixed the Enter key in Word Guess after tapping on-screen letters, and the daily guess no longer hangs when the server is slow. Fixed the streak hint notice looking squashed on Home. Hints also work in the dailies, where they are only recorded and never change your rank.
- **Switchyard** is redrawn as a toy railway: a meadow with trees and ponds, glowing tracks for the way the next train will go, and trains that move at the same smooth speed on every track. A depot lights up with a tick or a cross to show whether the train heading for it is the right one. Trains roll into the right depot with sparks and a bounce, and a wrong one jolts, smokes and costs a life, each with its own vibration.
- **Comet Slice** comets are now thrown in all directions but always on a path that stays on the screen, so none are lost out of view. It also draws much lighter, so it runs smoothly in a browser.
- Coins and hints are harder to earn: smaller coin rewards for the mind set, perfect day, boss levels and the daily chest, bigger daily quests, a hint stash that holds 6, and a better chance that the wheel pays one hint.
- The coin counter no longer jumps when "Waking server" appears, and the app only wakes the server at start when results have been waiting for a day (or have piled up).

Includes everything from 0.23.0.
