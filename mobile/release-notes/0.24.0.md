## Better
- **Switchyard** is redrawn as a toy railway: a meadow with trees and ponds, glowing tracks for the way the next train will go, and trains that move at the same smooth speed on every track. A depot lights up with a tick or a cross to show whether the train heading for it is the right one. Trains roll into the right depot with sparks and a bounce, and a wrong one jolts, smokes and costs a life, each with its own vibration.
- **Comet Slice** comets now bounce off the sides of the screen, so none are lost out of view. It also draws much lighter, so it runs smoothly in a browser.

Includes everything from 0.23.0.
