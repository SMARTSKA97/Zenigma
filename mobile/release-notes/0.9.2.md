## Vibration
- Small vibrations as you play in every game: a light tick for taps and steps, a firmer tap when a queen or word is placed, and distinct patterns for a mistake, a refused word and a win
- Turn it off in Settings under Appearance

## Mistakes
- When a full board is wrong, the warning now waits until you pause, like the highlights do, and any move cancels it

## Boards
- Bigger Word Guess tiles and keyboard
