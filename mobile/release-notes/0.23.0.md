## New
- **Switchyard**, a new mind game: real rails with sleepers, a tunnel gate, little locomotives and barn stations. Coloured trains roll down the main line; tap the round switches to send each one to a station of its own colour. Forks, sidings that rejoin the line, and stations in different places every round. Each round brings more trains, closer together and faster.

## Better
- Some games have new names, close to the old ones: Queens is now Crowns, Tango is Eclipse, Zip is Zipline, Patches is Patchwork, Pinpoint is Pindown and Crossclimb is Word Climb. Your progress and streaks are unchanged.
- Ranks is organised as Daily or Practice, then Puzzles or Mind games, then the game. Mind games have boards too: the daily set, the extra dailies, and each game's best marks in Practice.
- The result of a puzzle now shows how clean the solve was: no hints, no mistakes, or flawless when it is both. Word and mind games say it in their result too, and Chain Link and Word Climb have a Hint button now.
- Pindown shows your answer as letters, with a clear gap when you press space.
- Bottom sheets (like Streak freezes) can be put away: drag the handle down or tap it.
- Wend is one board per game now, like the other puzzle games. It is scored out of 100 (hints and a slow solve cost points), and each win makes the next board bigger.
- Chain Link starts with four links and grows to nine (it used to start with two). The daily chain is seven links.
- The Mind tab no longer repeats today's mind set. It lives in Challenges.
- Earning coins takes much more effort: levels pay from every 10th new level (bigger every 20th), a daily puzzle gives 1, the mind set 3 and a perfect day 5. The wheel pays less, and everything the app reports shares a 12 coin cap a day.
- Games use the whole screen: Word Guess stretches its board to the height of the phone, and Mind games spread out and grow on tall phones, tablets and unfolded foldables.
- Vibration is stronger everywhere (longer pulses, firmer wins, errors and misses), and the default for taps is now Strong.
- Motion and look borrowed from Orion Store (Material 3 Expressive): spring easing everywhere, buttons and cards squash to 94% when pressed and spring back, plain buttons change shape while pressed, lists rise in one after another, the new tab's icon pops, sheets rise with a spring, and every card, tab and button gives a small tick. Settings groups are now separate tiles with big outer corners, and cards have rounder corners.
- Livelier motion: springy button presses, page changes that slide in, confetti for a Word Guess win and a new best in Mind games, a bouncy score on the result card, and stronger shakes and pops for right and wrong answers.

## Fixed
- Opening a game no longer carries on waking the server: a wake-up already running stops once you start practising, and coming back online only wakes the server when results are waiting.
- Hints can be seen again with reduced motion on (the Wend hint is a ring now, not only a glow).
- Number Sort no longer scores 0 for a slow finish; finishing always earns points.
- The lucky wheel spins again (it did not animate when the phone asks for reduced motion).
- Zip with blocked cells: finishing the path now counts, and the timer stops.
- Zip with walls: the path and the walls are drawn together, so the path no longer drops out while you draw.

## Behind the scenes
- The app's colour themes are built without warnings now, and use the current Sass functions, so a future toolchain update will not break them.
