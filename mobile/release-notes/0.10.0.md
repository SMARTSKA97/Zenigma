## Level map
- Practice is now a map: a winding road of levels with your position marked, stars on solved levels, and a flame on the hard one that ends every ten
- Levels open one at a time: solve a level to unlock the next
- Tap any level for its difficulty, your best time and the time to beat for three stars; levels end with a star rating
- 1000 levels in every game, up from 200. Every group of ten climbs from easy to hard, and each group starts a little harder and ends a little harder than the one before it
- Everyone gets exactly the same puzzle on a given level
- Your progress carries over: you continue after the furthest level you solved (levels are rebalanced, so some levels are now different puzzles)

## Feel
- Vibration now works for every tap and for dragging crosses in Queens and typing in Word Guess
- Word Guess keys light up and show the letter as you press them
