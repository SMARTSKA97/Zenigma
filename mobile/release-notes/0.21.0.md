## New
- **Sign in with Google in the app.** "Continue with Google" now works on Android, using your phone's own account picker. You can also connect Google to an existing account from Account, and sign in with either your email or Google afterwards.

## Email
- Forgot your password? Get a 6-digit code by email and set a new one. You can also confirm your email in Account.

## Playing
- Messages such as "Not quite" now show in a fixed strip under the board, so the board no longer jumps.
- Queens and Tango wait a little longer before flagging a mistake, so you can fix it first.
- Saves only wake the server when it is already awake or when you open Ranks, Daily or Circles, so the app starts faster and results still sync.

## Security
- Stronger sign-in protection: sessions end when you change your password, stolen sign-in tokens are detected, and backups of app data are off.

## Good to know
- If Google sign-in says it did not work on this install, use your email for now; it only works on builds registered with Google.

Includes everything from 0.20.0.
