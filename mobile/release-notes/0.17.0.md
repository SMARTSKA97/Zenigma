Sudoku now has five difficulties, Easy, Medium, Hard, Challenging and Insane, for both Classic and Mini. Pick a kind, then a difficulty; each has its own levels, map and progress. Earlier Sudoku progress starts fresh.
Four new Sudoku kinds: Diagonal (both long diagonals hold 1 to 9), Windoku (four shaded 3×3 windows), Disjoint Groups (matching spots in every box) and Anti-Knight (no digit repeats a knight's move away).
More levels every week: every game and kind gains 1000 new levels each week, and levels load only when you reach them.
Speed round: five easy levels against a three-minute clock, with your best kept on this device.
Challenge a friend: sharing a practice result now includes a link to that level with your time to beat.
Home: continue the board you left, a recap of last week, which friends played today, last month's circle podium, and a warning when a streak is about to end.
Circles have monthly seasons with a podium. Results show how many players you were faster than.
Redo, plus Ctrl or Cmd + Z / Shift + Z on a keyboard. Skip ahead 5 levels after three quick wins in a row.
How to play opens on its own the first time you play a game, and every board now has a spoken description.
Settings redesigned in grouped sections. New: colour themes that unlock as you play, larger numbers, separate vibration strength for taps and for mistakes and wins, install as an app on the web.
Practice is split into Play and Coming soon, with the new games and kinds on the way. Toggle switches were rebuilt so they respond reliably.
Share your streak as a picture, new badges, and a countdown when the next daily set arrives.
