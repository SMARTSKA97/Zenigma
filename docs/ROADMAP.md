# Roadmap

Where Zenigma is going. These are plans, not promises: order and scope change with what players ask for and with the
time one person has. The detailed history and every idea (done, decided against, to do) is in [IDEAS.md](IDEAS.md).

**Standing rules:** playing never needs the server; hints and anything that could be bought never touch a ranking; no ads, no tracking.

## Now: 0.24

- Hints that show on every game, with a **Show me** that plays the next step
- Daily streak share card and a "still to play today" list
- Settings that work like a tablet's: list on the left, details on the right, in landscape too
- Pay by UPI with an app picker (Android), a QR code (web) and a "did it go through?" step
- Web and iPhone/iPad app updates with no server, and a picture guide to install on iOS
- Results come back after signing in on a new device

## Next: 0.25 to 0.27

| Theme | Plan |
|---|---|
| Play | More mind games, Patches **blocked cells** |
| Everywhere | An iPhone/iPad **streak-at-risk reminder** (installed web app notifications) and an icon badge |
| Reliability | Per-game daily results synced to the account (today only the day's total is) |
| Money | Tip links for more countries |

## Later: 0.28 and beyond

- **Home-screen widget** for Android (streak and today's dailies); iOS needs a native app, see below
- **Language packs** for Word Guess beyond English (Hindi, Bengali, Tamil first)
- **Level editor and community levels** with a review step
- **Native iOS app** (Capacitor) with a WidgetKit widget, if enough people ask and the Apple developer fee is covered
- **Automatic tip confirmation** through a payment gateway, instead of the owner checking each claim
- **Google Play release** (the signed bundle and data-safety answers are ready, see [PLAY_STORE.md](PLAY_STORE.md))

## Not planned

- Ads and tracking
- Anything bought that changes a ranking
- Offline dailies (a timed daily has to be checked by the server so times are fair)
