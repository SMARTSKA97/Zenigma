# Practice levels

Every practice track (a game, or a kind of a game) has levels that are generated by `tools/generators/puzzles` and shipped inside the apps and the API, so a solution can be checked against the exact level a player was given.

## How they are stored

- `tools/generators/puzzles/level-counts.json` says how many levels each track has. It is the one place to change.
- `node build-levels.mjs` writes **packs of 1000 levels**: `apps/web/public/levels/<track>-<n>.json` (fetched by the app when a player reaches that pack, cached for offline play) and the same files embedded in the API (`apps/api/.../Games/Puzzles/levels`, loaded the first time one of their levels is needed). `counts.json` and `apps/web/src/app/games/level-counts.data.ts` carry the counts.
- Levels never change once they exist: level *n* of a track is generated from the seed `zenigma-practice:<track>:<n>`. Building again only generates the levels that are missing.
- `node build-levels.mjs --all` throws everything away and generates every level again (slow: Zip's hardest levels take seconds each).
- `node build-levels.mjs --verify` is what CI runs: it checks every pack is complete and regenerates a sample (every 40th level and the last 10 of each track) to prove the shipped levels still match the generators.

## Adding levels every week

`node grow-levels.mjs` adds 1000 levels to every track (`level-counts.json`), generates them, and refreshes the test vectors. A scheduled task runs it weekly, runs the tests, pushes a branch, waits for CI and merges to `main`.

Difficulty: most tracks reach their hardest setting around level 500 and then repeat the hardest zone-of-ten ramp (easy to hard inside each zone of ten). The Sudoku tiers (Easy to Insane) ramp inside their own clue range.

## Tracks

`queens`, `tango`, `zip`, `patches`, `sudoku-<classic|mini>-<easy|medium|hard|challenging|insane>`, `sudoku-diagonal`, `sudoku-knight`.

To add a kind: add its generator case in `games.mjs` (`practiceLevel`), list it in `TRACKS` and `level-counts.json`, add it to `PuzzleRules.Tracks` (API) and to `VARIANTS` in `catalog.ts`, then run `node build-levels.mjs <track>` and `node gen-vectors.mjs`.
